You are a senior product designer, UX architect, and full-stack startup engineer.

Your task is to design and architect a cross-platform habit tracking application called “sisiGo”.

The product must include:
- A mobile app (iOS + Android)
- A desktop/web app
- Shared backend infrastructure
- Real-time sync
- Modern UI/UX
- Habit psychology and retention systems

The goal of sisiGo is to help users build consistency with minimal friction.

==================================================
PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

sisiGo should feel:
- Fast
- Calm
- Rewarding
- Minimal
- Emotional
- Visual
- Modern

Avoid:
- Productivity overload
- Corporate dashboard aesthetics
- Excessive gamification
- Cluttered interfaces

The app should prioritize:
1. One-tap habit completion
2. Beautiful streak visualization
3. Momentum and consistency
4. Fast daily interaction
5. Emotional reinforcement
6. Cross-device continuity

The experience should be:
- Simpler than Notion
